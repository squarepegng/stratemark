FAQ

Questions you might have.

What is Stratemark?

An AI market research tool. You describe a market; Stratemark researches it and organizes the companies into a structured deck you can explore, compare and keep researching.

How does Stratemark research a market?

It runs live, cited Google research on your own Gemini key — finding companies, verifying profiles, checking revenue and valuation signals, and scoring maturity tiers. You can watch each step complete.

Where does the information come from?

Public sources: company websites, earnings releases, filings, press and analyst coverage. Every metric keeps its citations, and each is labelled verified, estimated or unknown.

Can I verify the research?

Yes — that's the point. Click any metric's badge to open its fact-check panel and read the sources. "Verified" requires a citation with a named publisher, enforced in code.

How does Stratemark find companies?

From your market description it searches the live web for companies in scope, builds a profile for each, and adds context cards — infrastructure, distribution, insights and barriers to entry.

What is a deck?

Your market, organized: a structured set of company profiles plus market context, with consistent metrics so companies can actually be compared. It lives on your machine as a file you own.

Can I compare companies?

Yes. Select any cards in a deck and compare them side-by-side, or ask a grounded question about exactly those companies.

Can I ask follow-up questions?

Yes. The AI panel docks beside your deck. Answers come from the deck's stored research plus a fresh Google Search — never from model memory — with citations.

Who is Stratemark for?

Anyone who needs to understand a market: founders sizing an opportunity, teams doing competitive research, investors building an initial landscape, strategists looking for gaps.

Can I turn the research into a report?

Yes. A finished deck generates a market report — executive summary, company table, tiers and sources — and exports as PowerPoint (.pptx), PDF or Markdown.

How is it different from ChatGPT or Perplexity?

Chat gives you an answer that scrolls away. Stratemark gives you a structured market you can keep working with — consistent company profiles, side-by-side comparison, visible sources, and follow-up questions grounded in the same deck.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

What’s in the browser demo?

A working Stratemark interface with real sample market decks you can explore in the browser — cards, confidence badges, citations, and dashboards. Use it to feel the product before you install. When you want to research your own markets, clone the open-source app or use easy install (still bring your own key).

Is research really free to run?

Yes. Research runs on your own Google AI Studio / Gemini key. We never see your key and never proxy your traffic. Google’s free tier is enough for many decks; heavier use is between you and Google. Easy install does not include API credits.

How do I know the numbers aren’t hallucinated?

Every figure is tagged verified, estimated, or unknown. “Verified” requires evidence — a citation with a named publisher — and that rule is enforced in code. When nothing credible exists, the product shows an honest gap instead of a guess. Click any badge to see the receipts.

What’s in a deck?

A deck is a market turned into competitive-intelligence cards. Companies appear as scored cards with metrics, sources, and a maturity tier. Beyond company cards, decks can include related types — Infrastructure, Distribution, Culture, Vice (sourced controversies), Insight (market dynamics), and Barrier to Entry — so you’re not stuck with a flat list of logos. Open a company to go deeper: metrics, live intel, org context, and more.

What are the eight maturity tiers?

From T1 The Sandbox (pre-product, speculative R&D) through T8 The Titans (absolute market behemoths). In between: Scrappy Startups, Emerging Challengers, Growth Stage, Market Disruptors, Scale Stage, and Legacy Incumbents. Tiers come from a transparent scoring model — select a tier in the strip above for plain-language breakdowns.

Where does my data live?

On your machine. Your decks, research threads, and reports stay local. We don’t run a cloud vault of your research. Bring your own key means Google sees your research API calls — we don’t.

What’s the difference between open source and easy install?

Open source is free: clone the repo, bring your own key, run it yourself. Easy install is an optional one-time pay-what-you-want purchase ($1–100) for convenient packaging and setup. It still requires your own Gemini key. You’re supporting the team and a smoother install — not buying a hosted API plan.

Will there be a subscription later?

This path is one-time easy install + BYOK. If we offer a hosted or non-technical subscription later, it will be disclosed clearly at checkout with price, interval, and cancel terms — not slipped into this SKU.
